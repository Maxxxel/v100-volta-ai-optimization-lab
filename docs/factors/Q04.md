## Theory and Volta implications

AWQ uses activation-aware weight scaling/quantization. It needs matching weights, architecture support and an actual SM70 inference path.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

**Remaining scope:** First-MLP calibration data are available. Compensated weight/norm transform, supported-native-format quantization and independent modelquality/resource qualification are pending; all other layers remain uncalibrated for this statistic.

**Reopening condition:** Implement exact per-channel calibration/search and inverse scaling across all affected RMSNorm/GDN/attention consumers, prove tensor/numerical equivalence then quantize to a supported nativeformat. FreshGPUcalibration only for missing independent statistics; do not repeatR187.

**Next action:** Q04 next: admit exact first-MLP GGUF weight layout and inverse scaling of the stored post-attention normalization multiplier plus both gate/up input-column matrices. Reuse R397 fit chunks0/1 and untouched development2/3; do not recapture or use benchmark questions. Prove unquantized graph equivalence, then define one bounded native-format quantization proxy before any derived-model B-screen. No full-model AWQ, combinations, training or new-model comparison claim.
