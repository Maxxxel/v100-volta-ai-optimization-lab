## Theory and Volta implications

Retrieval selects relevant history with provenance. It can save prefill while omitting facts required by later tool decisions.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

R151 exakte Asset-ID-/Policy-Quellenwahl: 12/12 gegen 8/12, 15,955 gegen 123,081 s, GPU-J 4135,82 gegen 32983,62 im engen frischen Aufgabenpaar. R188 unabhängige HA/PVE-Fälle nur 5/12 gegen 3/12; absolutes Qualitätsgate verfehlt. Keine Embedding-/Vector-RAG- oder LLM-Summary-Messung. R404 BMT07 screen recorded separately: 4/12 correct abstentions in B vs 0/12 in A0; A0 made four false tool calls on negative/ambiguous cases, B made none. All eight positive calculations failed in both arms, so independent balanced confirmation is required and no broad retrieval-quality claim is made.

**Remaining scope:** R404 independent balanced confirmation; positive arithmetic remained 0/8 both arms. Vector RAG still needs a pinned licensed embedding/index, update/RAM budget and independent paraphrase/provenance tasks. LLM summarization needs a separate fact/authority contract.

**Reopening condition:** Exakte ID-Auswahl nicht als semantischen Retriever ausgeben. Vektorsuche erfordert gepinntes lizenziertes Embedding-/Index-Artefakt, RAM/VRAM-/Updatebudget, Paraphrasen und Provenienz sowie vollständige Downstream-Aufgabenkosten. Zusammenfassung benötigt eigenen Fakten-/Authority-Vertrag und unabhängige Bewertung.

**Next action:** Run a fresh, balanced confirmation on independent synthetic fixtures. Keep the same isolated source-context treatment and strict scoring; inspect positive arithmetic and negative abstention separately. If positive correctness remains 0/8 in both arms, report only the safety/latency scope and do not claim general agent success. Vector RAG and LLM summarization stay deferred pending separately pinned components and contracts.

These canonical rationale excerpts preserve the original language. Potential combinations require independently qualified constituents and fresh interaction controls. Transfer to another model requires compatible architecture, weights, tokenizer, state layout and engine dispatch.
