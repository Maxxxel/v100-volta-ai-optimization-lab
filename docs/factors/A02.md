## Theory and Volta implications

Retrieval selects relevant history with provenance. It can save prefill while omitting facts required by later tool decisions.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

R151 exakte Asset-ID-/Policy-Quellenwahl: 12/12 gegen 8/12, 15,955 gegen 123,081 s, GPU-J 4135,82 gegen 32983,62 im engen frischen Aufgabenpaar. R188 unabhängige HA/PVE-Fälle nur 5/12 gegen 3/12; absolutes Qualitätsgate verfehlt. Keine Embedding-/Vector-RAG- oder LLM-Summary-Messung. R404 BMT07 screen recorded separately: 4/12 correct abstentions in B vs 0/12 in A0; A0 made four false tool calls on negative/ambiguous cases, B made none. All eight positive calculations failed in both arms, so independent balanced confirmation is required and no broad retrieval-quality claim is made. R405 independently replicated the narrow synthetic abstention/latency screen: A0 0/12 strict, B 4/12 (four correct abstentions), positive calculations 0/8 both, no B false calls; total request latency 71.04% lower in this workload. No broad retrieval-quality claim or champion promotion.

**Remaining scope:** A02 remains PARTIAL. BMT07 safe-abstention/latency scope is measured but positive calculations were 0/8 in both R404 and R405 arms. Embedding-backed vector RAG and model-generated summaries remain separate DEFERRED variants with prerequisites in A02_VARIANT_ADMISSION_20261022.json.

**Reopening condition:** Exakte ID-Auswahl nicht als semantischen Retriever ausgeben. Vektorsuche erfordert gepinntes lizenziertes Embedding-/Index-Artefakt, RAM/VRAM-/Updatebudget, Paraphrasen und Provenienz sowie vollständige Downstream-Aufgabenkosten. Zusammenfassung benötigt eigenen Fakten-/Authority-Vertrag und unabhängige Bewertung.

**Next action:** A03 tool-schema/harness compression: inspect only a distinct candidate that preserves all disambiguating permissions and complete strict tool quality; do not replay R155/R191.
