## Theory and Volta implications

A selected upstream kernel port must identify an actual algorithm or launch-layout change. A newer revision number alone is not an optimization.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

Zwei gepinnte Q6-Ports auf Hardware geprüft: Pascal-MMQ verschlechtert 2K/4K-Latenz um 11,23/11,43 % unter gemeinsam erzwungenem Routing. Vier-Zeilen-MMVQ: Decode −0,107 %, kein praktisch relevanter Gewinn. Historisches QUALIFIED bedeutet hier Protokollprüfung, keinen Champion.

**Remaining scope:** Ausgewählte Upstreampatches haben Pfad-/Numerikbelege, teils deutliche Prefillregression. Neue Revisionsnummer ist kein Verfahren.

**Reopening condition:** Nur konkreten bislang nicht enthaltenen semantisch kompatiblen Patch mit erwartbarem Nutzen/ausgeführtem Pfad zulassen; existierende Artefakte wiederverwenden.

**Next action:** Need a specific not-yet-contained patch, executed compatible tensor shape, independent oracle and expected practical benefit. Revision updates or repeated tested row/config ports do not qualify.

These canonical rationale excerpts preserve the original language. Potential combinations require independently qualified constituents and fresh interaction controls. Transfer to another model requires compatible architecture, weights, tokenizer, state layout and engine dispatch.
