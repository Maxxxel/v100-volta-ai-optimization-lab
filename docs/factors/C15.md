## Theory and Volta implications

Fatbinary compression affects distribution and loading. The effective NVCC flag must be present; a CMake cache entry or different ELF hash is insufficient.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

This is actual partialCPUartifact evidence; fullbackend/runtime loader, startup, taskquality/latency/VRAM/energy remain untested. Existing unstableR386 startup control is not automatically reusable. ParentPARTIAL is a scope-classification clarification, not GPUcoverage or a performance qualification. No rebuild or new modelstart.

**Remaining scope:** Effective single-objectcompression proved; no fullbackend pack/loading or GPU result. Completebackend treatment and stableloaderprotocol stillrequired before any speedclaim. Oneobjectsize isnotVRAM or fullengine size.

**Reopening condition:** Do notchangeA0/toolkit; admit explicitcompression-onlybuild onlyafter artifact/resource/loader-benefit gates.

**Next action:** Preserve boundedCPUcompressionproof; no compressionmodegrid. Reopen fullbackend/runtime test for a justified loader/storage usecase with stable timing conditions, independentquality and nativeimage identity.
