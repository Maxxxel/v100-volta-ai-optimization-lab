## Theory and Volta implications

LoRA/QLoRA train adapters with reduced optimizer state. The exact hybrid/MTP implementation and a real training step must fit the device.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

A native experimentaltrainer exists, but its supplied optimizer marksF32modeltensors, not a demonstrated trainableLoRAadapter path; systemPython has no torch/transformers/peft/bitsandbytes. Otherprivateenvironments are not excluded.

**Remaining scope:** Demonstrate actual trainableadapter registration, compatibleQwen35GDN/autograd or a correctly frozen-backbone path, private data split and one boundedFP16/SM70optimizerstep with measuredmemory. No blanketclaimthatQLoRAcannotfit16GB.
