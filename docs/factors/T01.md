## Theory and Volta implications

LoRA/QLoRA train adapters with reduced optimizer state. The exact hybrid/MTP implementation and a real training step must fit the device.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

Native reduced output-LoRA graph has passed CPU-only MSE and cross-entropy optimizer checks at actual Omni dimensions. The mixed graph uses F32 master A/B parameters (required by the pinned AdamW kernels) and explicit F16 forward casts; the analytic gradient matched exactly in both latest CE and MSE checks. Pinned CUDA source supports the required operation families, but actual V100 dispatch, adapter GGUF loading, model-derived features and measured training memory remain unverified. R398 and R399 are now reconciled: R398 captured finite train/dev result_norm after exact control-logit checks but aborted on pinned scheduler contract before optimizer; R399 fixed the CPU-last scheduler order and invoked optimizer evaluation, but the optimizer output backend was not proven CUDA. No optimizer/gradient pass, adapter GGUF load, training-quality or deployment result. Preserve both GPU sessions as partial evidence; never rerun unchanged. Independent source/raw review: R399 checks the backend of the forward logits tensor `out`, not directly the GGML_OP_OPT_STEP_ADAMW node or its gradient. The pinned CUDA source does implement/support that op, but R399 does not prove actual V100 assignment. Keep OPEN and do not repeat unchanged.

**Remaining scope:** Freeze adapter GGUF metadata/shapes and isolated R187-derived train/dev token IDs. On GPU capture result_norm for those selected positions, train one bounded cross-entropy step with F32 masters/F16 forward, verify a saved adapter loads into the pinned model, and measure real VRAM/backend placement. No quality claim from one step.

**Reopening condition:** Demonstrate actual trainableadapter registration, compatibleQwen35GDN/autograd or a correctly frozen-backbone path, private data split and one boundedFP16/SM70optimizerstep with measuredmemory. No blanketclaimthatQLoRAcannotfit16GB.

**Next action:** Freeze a direct optimizer-step node/backend and gradient observer, then pass CPU build and broker preflight before any new one-shot; no unchanged R398/R399 retry.
