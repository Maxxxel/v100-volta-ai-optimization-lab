## Theory and Volta implications

An independent tool gate checks authorization and arguments outside generation. A safe simulator result does not authorize production actions.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

R192/R241 native GPU-Vorschläge und logische Gateprüfung getrennt: R241 zwölf unzulässige Vorschläge, alle blockiert; nur3/18 erlaubte Aufgaben mit korrektem Call. Keine tatsächlichen Tool-Ausführungen. Feld-/Typ-/Envelopeprüfung ist eine Komponente, keine bewiesene umfassende Autorisierung oder injectionsicheres Modell.

**Remaining scope:** Current R192/R241 synthetic proposal/gateway evidence and R400 CPU component checks retained. Policy provenance/revocation remain DEFERRED until a real isolated versioned authority/receiver contract exists. No additional high-stakes scenario is admitted without a distinct contract or threat model.

**Reopening condition:** Erforderlich für neue Safety-/Recoveryvariante: unabhängige Policies, Widerruf/Provenienz, Typ-/Bereichsfehler und erlaubte Calls einschließlich Fehlblockierungen prüfen. Keine realen HA-/[REDACTED]. Vorhandene24er-Injectionprobe nicht wiederholen oder als breite Modellqualität ausgeben.

**Next action:** Do not replay existing fixtures or run production actions. Reopen only with a versioned policy authority/receiver test contract; proceed to other prioritized singles.

These canonical rationale excerpts preserve the original language. Potential combinations require independently qualified constituents and fresh interaction controls. Transfer to another model requires compatible architecture, weights, tokenizer, state layout and engine dispatch.
