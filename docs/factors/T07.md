## Theory and Volta implications

Tensor smoothing redistributes activation/weight ranges. It changes tensor semantics and needs architecture-aware calibration and numerical checks.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

Native second moments do not determine per-channel maxima/absoluteactivationdistribution. No actualsmoothing scale+all-consumer-compensation artifact is admitted. This is not a blankethardwareincompatibility.

**Remaining scope:** Choose a definedsmoothingalgorithm/deploymentprecision, collect missing nonbenchmarkactivations, fold scales into allgraphconsumers correctly and prove numericalequivalence before quality/performanceGPUtests. Keep modelweightchanges separate from runtimeflags.
