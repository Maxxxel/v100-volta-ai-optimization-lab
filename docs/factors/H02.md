## Theory and Volta implications

Host CPU isolation affects other services and needs an explicitly isolated environment. It cannot be justified by an unmeasured inference benefit.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

All six host CPUs are available to the CT; per-process affinity is already measured in R18/R363. Exclusive host CPU isolation is a different treatment and would require reserving cores away from productive services/Proxmox.

**Remaining scope:** Dedicated nonproductive CPU/host resource reservation and measured contention question; existing private process-affinity data cannot certify exclusivity.
