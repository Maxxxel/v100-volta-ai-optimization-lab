## Theory and Volta implications

Fused dequantization and matrix products avoid materialized intermediates. Existing fused defaults must not be presented as a newly achieved speedup.

On SM70, require an actually dispatched compatible kernel and include conversion, workspace, launch and verification costs. The 16 GB budget includes weights, attention KV, recurrent states, graph pools and transient allocations. Quality, end-to-end latency and energy per successful task take precedence over a token-throughput screen.

## Decision and remaining research

Unfused-Kontrolle gegen erzwungene MMVQ/MMQ-Fusion: großer Decode-Unterschied, aber 2K/4K-Latenz +43,96/+49,95 %. Standard nutzt bereits passende Fusionspfade; kein Gewinn gegenüber Stock-A0.

**Remaining scope:** Der alte Vergleich dequant_then_cublas→fused erreicht14,65→79,68Decode-t/s, aber gegen künstlich entfuzte Kontrolle; globale Fusion verschlechtert Prefill. Der Standard nutzt bereits passende Fusionspfade.

**Reopening condition:** Keinen4x-Standard-Speedup ableiten. Nur tatsächlich neue Fusion mit anderem ausgeführtem Kernel und gültiger Stockreferenz zulassen; HBM06-Alias übernehmen.

**Next action:** Need an actually unfused current hot path plus a distinct semantically equivalent implemented fusion. Existing MMVQ/MMQ and HBM07 conversion reuse are not a new fusion.

These canonical rationale excerpts preserve the original language. Potential combinations require independently qualified constituents and fresh interaction controls. Transfer to another model requires compatible architecture, weights, tokenizer, state layout and engine dispatch.
